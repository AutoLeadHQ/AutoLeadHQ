Every missed call is a coin flip

A homeowner with no heat or water on the floor doesn't leave a voicemail. They call the next contractor on the list, and whoever answers first gets the job.

  • Your techs are under a house or on a roof, not by the phone.
  • Calls after 5pm and on weekends hit voicemail, and that's when a lot of emergencies happen.
  • One lost no-heat call can be a repair, a maintenance customer for years, or a full system replacement.

Questions

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. Your number stays the same. You turn on "forward when busy or unanswered" to your AutoLeadHQ number, and we walk you through it on a short call.

What if I answer the call?

Then nothing happens. AutoLeadHQ only steps in when the call isn't picked up.

Will customers know it's automated?

The texts come from your shop's name and read like a person at your shop. If a customer asks, it says it's your shop's text assistant. It never pretends to be a person.

Is there a contract?

Month to month. If it isn't bringing you jobs, cancel.
